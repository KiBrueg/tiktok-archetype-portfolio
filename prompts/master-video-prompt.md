# Master Video Prompt (Current Best Version)

Use this as the base for almost all animations.  
It is optimized for:

- Locked static camera
- No zoom / no Ken Burns / no still-image enlargement
- Fluid continuous motion
- 8–13 second seamless loop potential
- Hypnotic go-go style

```
Locked static camera, completely fixed, no zoom, no push-in, no Ken Burns, no still-image enlargement, no camera movement. Exact same woman, face, hair, body, outfit and setting as the reference image.

She performs a smooth continuous hypnotic go-go dance for loop: slow rhythmic hip rolls and isolations, soft full-body waves, gentle upper-body sway in time with the music. Movements are fluid, natural, flowing and endless — never sharp, never abrupt, never frozen.

She keeps strong direct eye contact with the camera. Brief soft corner-of-the-lips smile, then immediately returns to calm arrogant poker face. Slight lean of her upper body closer toward the camera. Soft upper-body shake. Hands stay only on her own body — lightly outlining and resting on her waist and hips.

8-13 seconds of pure continuous body motion, perfect for seamless loop. No cuts, no freezes, no effects.
```

### Fallback version (if the tool flags content)

Replace the hands sentence with:

```
Hands rest on her own waist and hips.
```

### Notes

- Always keep the camera instructions at the very beginning.
- The expression sequence (brief smile → poker face) is important for the hypnotic effect.
- Do not add extra actions (no blowing kisses, no reaching toward camera, no finger gestures).

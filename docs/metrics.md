# Metrics We Track

## Primary Performance Metrics

| Metric                        | Why it matters                              | Good signal                  |
|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Average Watch %               | Core retention indicator                    | > 65–70%                     |
| % of viewers who finish video | Strong loop / rewatch potential             | High                         |
| Likes per 1000 views          | Basic engagement                            | Above account baseline       |
| Saves per 1000 views          | High intent / “want to rewatch”             | Strong positive              |
| Shares per 1000 views         | Viral coefficient                           | Any meaningful lift          |
| Profile visits per 1000 views | Interest in the “character” / account       | Key conversion metric        |
| Comments quality              | “Who is she?”, “more”, language requests    | Qualitative gold             |

## Secondary / Business Metrics

- Donation amount & frequency (if donation marketing is active)
- Link clicks (bio or product)
- Follow rate from the video
- Audience retention graph shape (where people drop off)

## How We Compare Tests

We primarily look at **relative performance** against:

1. Account baseline (previous 14–30 days average)
2. Direct A/B pairs (same music, different archetype or vice versa)
3. Absolute thresholds that historically correlated with further growth

## Notes

- Early views (first 200–500) are noisy — we wait for at least 1–2k views before drawing strong conclusions when possible.
- Time of posting and current account temperature are always logged.

# Visual Archetypes Under Test

Each archetype is defined by a combination of body type, facial energy, hair, styling, and typical setting.

## 1. Soft Curvy – Dark Glamour
- Body: Soft hourglass, large breasts, wide hips, soft midsection
- Face: Calm / slightly arrogant, strong direct eye contact
- Hair: Long dark wavy or straight
- Typical outfits: Black sequin mini dress, latex, metallic
- Settings: Dark club, luxury lounge with money & champagne, BMW visible
- Energy: Hypnotic, controlled, high-status

## 2. Soft Curvy – Metallic Club
- Same body type
- Outfits: Shiny silver, purple, red metallic bikini or bodysuit
- Setting: Stage or DJ booth with strong colored lights
- Energy: More performance-oriented, still hypnotic

## 3. Soft Curvy – Beach / Sunset DJ
- Same body proportions
- Outfit: White or light micro bikini + open shirt, headphones, sunglasses
- Setting: Beach party at golden hour
- Energy: Freer, warmer, still highly physical

## 4. Soft Curvy – Pool / Editorial
- Fuller soft figure, wet skin
- Red micro bikini, headphones
- Magazine-cover lighting and composition
- Energy: Static but high-impact (often used as thumbnail style)

## 5. Braided / Latina-leaning Curvy
- Long braids with gold accents
- Velvet or metallic outfits
- Strong eye contact, slightly more attitude

## 6. Anime-Kawaii Control
- Stylized, pink hair, cat ears, school-skirt look
- Used as a control / different audience segment
- Not the main focus of the current testing phase

---

**Current focus of experiments:** Archetypes 1, 2 and 3.  
The goal is to measure which combination of body presentation + outfit material + setting + music produces the strongest retention and profile-visit rates.
